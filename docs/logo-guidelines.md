*Version 1.0.0 — Comprehensive Mark Anatomy & Usage Rules*

## 1. Logo Concept & Typographic Heritage

The **iNESTYL OUTLET** identity is a custom geometric stencil wordmark engineered with architectural cuts, parallel negative-space channels, and rhythmic floating elements. It preserves the distinctive visual character of the original brand identity while adding the elegant **OUTLET** subtitle treatment flanked by symmetrical Champagne Gold rules.

```
       ┌─┐ (Champagne Gold Square Dot: #C6A15B)
       └─┘
        │   \ \        ───  ───\  ───┬───  \   /  │
        │    \ \       ───   \    ───┼───   \ /   │
        │     \ \      ───  ───/     │       │    └───
     ────────────────   O U T L E T   ────────────────
     (Champagne Gold)                  (Champagne Gold)
```

## 2. Anatomical Construction & Geometric Features

Every character in the mark follows precise mathematical alignments:

### 1. The Lowercase 'i' with Champagne Gold Square Dot
- **The Dot**: An exact square rendered exclusively in **Champagne Gold (`#C6A15B`)**.
- **Vertical Spacing**: Positioned detached above the vertical stem with a balanced negative-space gap.
- **The Stem**: A clean, unadorned vertical rectangle in Primary Black (`#111111`) or Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Prestige Significance**: The gold square dot is the primary visual anchor and core brand accent of iNESTYL OUTLET. It must never be altered, rounded into a circle, or rendered in black.

### 2. The Letter 'N'
- **Diagonal Slash**: A continuous 45-degree diagonal bar extending from the top-left vertex down to the bottom-right baseline.
- **Parallel Cutouts**: The left vertical stem terminates with an upward 45-degree slope; the right vertical stem terminates with a downward 45-degree slope.
- **Negative-Space Channels**: Two uniform 20px channels flank the diagonal stroke, giving the letter dynamic forward momentum.

### 3. The Letter 'E'
- **Floating Triple Bars**: The letter 'E' contains **no vertical stem**. It is formed from three parallel horizontal bars of equal width, height, and spacing.
- **Open Silhouette**: This architectural opening reinforces modern luxury minimalism.

### 4. The Letter 'S'
- **Geometric Balance**: A smooth, symmetrical sans-serif curve featuring precise terminal cuts and balanced counter spaces.

### 5. The Letter 'T'
- **Detached Crossbar**: The top horizontal crossbar floats detached above the central vertical stem, maintaining rhythmic consistency with the open gaps of 'E' and 'i'.

### 6. The Letter 'Y'
- **Angular Symmetry**: Two diagonal upper arms meeting at a precise apex above a centered vertical lower stem.

### 7. The Letter 'L'
- **Detached Base**: The vertical stem floats above the horizontal bottom base bar with an intentional negative-space gap, completing the stencil motif.

### 8. The OUTLET Subtitle Line
- **Typographic Treatment**: Set in widely-tracked geometric uppercase lettering (`O U T L E T`).
- **Symmetrical Gold Rules**: Flanked on the left and right by 3.5px rounded Champagne Gold rules that span outward to match the exact outer width of `iNESTYL` above it (from the left edge of 'i' to the right edge of 'L').

## 4. Clear Space & Exclusion Zone

To preserve visual authority, maintain a mandatory clear space around all logos:

```
          ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
          │                      1.5X                     │
          │         ┌───────────────────────────┐         │
   1.5X   │  1.5X   │      iNESTYL OUTLET       │   1.5X  │   1.5X
          │         └───────────────────────────┘         │
          │                      1.5X                     │
          └───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Reference Unit ($X$)**: The exact height of the Champagne Gold square dot above the lowercase 'i'.
- **Safe Zone**: Maintain at least **$1.5X$** clearance on all four sides of the mark.
- No typography, photographs, borders, or buttons may encroach within this zone.

## 6. Brand Misuse & Prohibited Practices

To protect identity integrity, the following practices are strictly prohibited:

- ❌ **NEVER** recolor the Champagne Gold square dot to black, red, or white.
- ❌ **NEVER** round the gold square dot into a circle.
- ❌ **NEVER** skew, stretch, condense, or italicize the mark.
- ❌ **NEVER** rearrange the letterforms or alter their relative proportions.
- ❌ **NEVER** remove the gold rules flanking `OUTLET` in the stacked mark.
- ❌ **NEVER** apply drop shadows, 3D embosses, glows, or gradients to the vector mark.
- ❌ **NEVER** place the black logo directly over complex, high-contrast, or colorful photographs without an ivory background container.
