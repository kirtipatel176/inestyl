# iNESTYL OUTLET — Photography & Visual Asset Guidelines
*Version 1.1.0 — Editorial Creative Direction & Image Pipeline*

---

## 1. Creative Direction: Editorial Fashion × Authentic Footwear

The photography style for **iNESTYL OUTLET** balances high-fashion editorial poise (inspired by Celine, Dior, and Prada lookbooks) with authentic sneaker culture and athletic craftsmanship (inspired by NikeLab, adidas Consortium, and HOKA).

Every photograph must establish:
1. **Unquestioned Authenticity**: 100% original manufacturer articles with authentic stitching, badges, and sole construction.
2. **Tactile Material Quality**: Clear visibility of full-grain leather, breathable mesh weaves, raw denim textures, and ribbed knit collars.
3. **Calm Architectural Elegance**: Neutral limestone, warm concrete plinths, and warm ivory backdrops matching the brand canvas (`#F4EFE7`).

---

## 2. Core Photography Principles

| Standard | Required Execution | Strict Prohibitions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lighting** | Soft, directional natural daylight (3500K–4500K) or diffuse studio lighting casting organic, soft contact shadows. | Hard flash glares, blown-out specular highlights, harsh colored gels, neon reflections. |
| **Color Balance** | Warm neutral tones matching `#F4EFE7`; realistic product color reproduction. | Overly saturated filters, cold blue clinical sweeps, heavy vignette borders. |
| **Backdrops** | Warm ivory, off-white, light sandstone plinths, textured travertine, architectural concrete. | Distracting urban graffiti, busy store floors, fake 3D gradients, stock-photo sweeps. |
| **Proportions** | True-to-life geometric proportions with minimal optical distortion (85mm–105mm prime lens equivalent). | Wide-angle lens stretching, unrealistic AI sneaker warping, fake branding or altered logos. |
| **Authenticity** | Genuine brand packaging, authorized brand product assets, real manufacturer tags. | Low-res counterfeit mockups, watermarked catalogue rips, plastic mannequins. |

## 3. Specific Image Category Guidelines

### A. Hero Banners & Homepage Campaigns
- **Concept**: Curated multi-brand arrangement (e.g. A Nike Air Max sneaker staged on a sandstone plinth alongside a folded stack of Levi's raw denim and a folded beige Nike sweatshirt).
- **Aspect Ratio**: `16:9` (Desktop: `1920×1080px` or `2400×1350px`) / `4:5` (Mobile: `1080×1350px`).
- **Composition**: Rule-of-thirds balance with ample clean negative space on the left side to accommodate bold typography and CTA buttons.

### B. Category Showcase Cards
- **Footwear Card**: Profile 3/4 angled view of an iconic trainer resting on a minimal stone block over Warm Ivory (`#F4EFE7`). Aspect ratio: `4:3` or `16:10` (`800×500px`).
- **Apparel Card**: High-end editorial lifestyle photography of models wearing authentic denim jackets, clean hoodies, and minimal streetwear. Aspect ratio: `4:3` or `16:10` (`800×500px`).

### C. Product Discovery & Detail Photography (5-Shot Library)
For every product indexed in the catalogue, adhere to a standardized 5-shot angle library:
1. **Shot 1 (Hero Catalogue View)**: Outer lateral profile (side angle), facing right, clean silhouette cutout over Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm Ivory Tint (`#FAF8F5`) with soft contact drop shadow.
2. **Shot 2 (Front / Toebox)**: Top-down 45-degree angle displaying lacing, tongue branding, and toebox mesh.
3. **Shot 3 (Heel / Rear)**: Rear view highlighting heel counter, air unit, and heel pull tab.
4. **Shot 4 (Outsole / Traction)**: Flat bottom view showing rubber outsole tread pattern and grip pods.
5. **Shot 5 (Tactile Macro Detail)**: Close-up macro showing leather grain, stitch density, or reflective accents.

### D. Featured Brand Cards
- **Dimensions**: `600×400px` (Desktop) / `400×300px` (Mobile).
- **Treatment**: Minimalist black rounded cards with subtle embossed brand logos or atmospheric campaign textures (e.g. mountain ridge for HOKA, raw denim for Levi's, running track for Nike).

### E. Flagship Store Photography (Kapadwanj Outlet)
- **Interior**: Wide architectural captures showing clean, uncluttered shoe display shelves, apparel racks, and warm 3000K ambient boutique lighting.
- **Exterior**: Daylight and illuminated evening captures of the `iNESTYL OUTLET` storefront in Kapadwanj, Gujarat.

## 4. Technical Delivery & Image CDN Pipeline

- **Next-Gen Formats**: WebP (Primary) and AVIF (Modern browsers), with JPEG fallback.
- **Cloudinary Optimization**: Automatic format (`f_auto`) and quality compression (`q_auto:good`) dynamically applied.
- **Max File Sizes**: Catalogue images $\le 250\text{ KB}$, Hero banners $\le 450\text{ KB}$, Thumbnails $\le 45\text{ KB}$.
