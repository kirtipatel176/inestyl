# iNESTYL OUTLET — Brand Identity Guidelines
*Version 1.1.0 — Phase 1 Complete Foundation*

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Purpose

**iNESTYL OUTLET** is a premier multi-brand footwear and apparel destination operating in **Kapadwanj, Gujarat**. The brand connects fashion-conscious consumers and sneaker enthusiasts with 100% authentic products from world-leading footwear and apparel giants at attractive outlet value.

### Brand Positioning Statement
> **"Original Brands. Outlet Prices. Premium Experience."**
> 
> iNESTYL OUTLET merges the calm, editorial prestige of high-fashion houses (Prada, Celine, Dior) with the bold, kinetic energy of modern athletic performance (Nike, adidas). The brand dispels the myth that outlet shopping must feel like a chaotic bargain basement, providing an elevated, gallery-grade discovery experience.

---

## 2. Business Overview & Brand Portfolio

### Core Product Brands
- **Footwear**: Nike, Puma, adidas, HOKA, and curated premium trainers.
- **Apparel**: Levi's, DIESEL, U.S. Polo Assn., and seasonal designer streetwear.

### Target Customer Segments
- **Style-Conscious Young Adults (18–35)**: Seeking authentic sneakers, premium denim, and streetwear drops to elevate their daily wardrobe.
- **Athletes & Performance Enthusiasts**: Looking for high-tier running (HOKA, adidas Boost, Nike Air) and training footwear at accessible outlet prices.
- **Value-Driven Discerning Shoppers**: Customers who demand genuine manufacturer quality and warranty over counterfeit alternatives.

---

## 3. Strict Non-E-Commerce Business Model

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **NO SHOPPING CART — NO CHECKOUT — NO ONLINE PAYMENTS**
> 
> iNESTYL OUTLET is strictly an **online product discovery catalogue, inventory index, and store enquiry portal**.
> 
> The platform deliberately omits:
> - Shopping Cart / "Add to Cart" buttons
> - Cart icon in header navigation
> - "Buy Now" triggers
> - Checkout funnels
> - Payment gateways (Razorpay, Stripe, PayU, UPI QR online processing)
> - Order history or customer payment accounts
> 
> **Every product interaction leads to:**
> 1. **Direct WhatsApp Enquiry** with store specialists for live size checking and reservations.
> 2. **Physical Store Visits** to our flagship outlet in Kapadwanj, Gujarat for in-person try-ons and authentic retail service.

## 6. Physical Packaging & Retail Touchpoints

The visual identity flows seamlessly from the digital discovery catalogue into physical customer packaging:

### A. Luxury Shopping Bags
- **Material**: Heavyweight 240gsm matte textured Warm Ivory craft paper (`#F4EFE7`).
- **Handles**: Matte black herringbone woven cotton ribbon.
- **Artwork**: Centered `iNESTYL OUTLET` primary stacked mark in matte black ink, with a hot-stamped Champagne Gold foil square dot above the 'i' and gold rules flanking `OUTLET`.
- **Base Gusset**: Debossed store location: `KAPADWANJ, GUJARAT`.
- **Vector Mockup Reference**: [`public/brand/packaging/shopping-bag-mockup.svg`](file:///Users/kirtipatel/Downloads/Colleage/IneStyl/public/brand/packaging/shopping-bag-mockup.svg).

### B. Product Hang Tags / Price Tags
- **Dimensions**: 50mm × 110mm vertical card tag.
- **Stock**: 350gsm uncoated Warm Ivory cardstock with die-cut eyelet and black string tie.
- **Front Face**: Primary mark with gold foil accent, "100% Original Authentic Product".
- **Back Face (Outlet Pricing Grid)**:
  - Brand name and SKU / Model identifier.
  - Size indicator (UK / EU / US).
  - Strikethrough Retail MRP (`MRP ₹12,995`).
  - Bold Outlet Price (`₹8,995`).
  - Champagne Gold savings pill (`YOU SAVE 31%`).
  - Scannable WhatsApp QR code linking directly to store customer support.

## 7. Tone of Voice & Copywriting Standards

- **Confident, Calm & Editorial**: Use short, declarative sentences. Avoid pushy sales jargon ("Hurry! Huge discount!", "Blink and you miss it!").
- **Authenticity-Centered**: Always reinforce that styles are authentic, brand-new, and sourced from verified distribution channels.
- **Clear Action Drivers**:
  - Preferred: *"Enquire on WhatsApp"*, *"Check Store Availability"*, *"Experience in Store"*, *"Explore Collection"*.
  - Prohibited: *"Add to Cart"*, *"Buy Now"*, *"Checkout"*, *"Place Order"*.
